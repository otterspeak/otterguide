What is RSVP?

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation explained

Understanding RSVP Reading

RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) is a speed reading technique where words are displayed one at a time in the same location on the screen. Instead of your eyes moving across lines of text, the words come to you - eliminating the time spent on eye movements (called "saccades").

No Eye Movement Required

Traditional reading requires your eyes to jump from word to word. RSVP eliminates this by presenting words in a fixed position, reducing eye fatigue and increasing reading efficiency.

Reduced Subvocalization

Subvocalization is the habit of "speaking" words in your head while reading. At higher WPM speeds, RSVP helps train your brain to recognize words without this internal narration.

Improved Focus

With only one word visible at a time, distractions are minimized. Your brain focuses entirely on processing each word as it appears.

Gradual Speed Training

Start at a comfortable WPM (150-200) and gradually increase. Over time, your brain adapts to faster speeds while maintaining comprehension.

Architecture V1

The Acoustic Intelligence Behind OtterSpeak

Otter Brain is the specialized, high-performance Artificial Intelligence engine developed exclusively to power OtterSpeak.

While most speech tools simply convert voice to text, Otter Brain was engineered with a more complex goal: to act as a real-time language coach. It doesn't just listen to what you say; it analyzes how you say it, assessing fluency, pronunciation accuracy, and pacing in milliseconds.

Why We Built It

Standard speech recognition APIs (like Google or Siri) are designed to be "forgiving"—they auto-correct your mistakes to guess your intent. This is great for ordering pizza, but terrible for learning a language.

We needed a system that was strict, precise, and honest.

Developed internally for the OtterSpeak ecosystem, Otter Brain serves as an unbiased judge. It uses deep acoustic modeling to compare a learner's speech against native-level phonetics, identifying exactly where a student stumbles, pauses too long, or mispronounces a sound.

How It Works

1
The Ear (Acoustic Decoding)

Using advanced deep learning architectures (based on Wav2Vec 2.0 technology), the model breaks down audio waves into raw phonetic probability vectors, capturing the tiniest nuances of sound 16,000 times per second.

2
The Aligning Engine (Forced Alignment)

Instead of just transcribing, Otter Brain performs "Forced Alignment." It takes the reference text (what the user should have said) and mathematically maps it onto the user's audio. This allows us to pinpoint the exact millisecond a specific word started and ended.

3
The Judge (Scoring Logic)

This is our custom proprietary logic. The Brain calculates a "Confidence Score" for every single word. It applies dynamic penalties for:

  • Latency: Taking too long to say a word.
  • Stuttering: Repeating sounds.
  • Mispronunciation: Substituting phonemes (e.g., saying "wabbit" instead of "rabbit").

Key Capabilities

Phonetic Precision

Understands speech at the phoneme level, allowing granular feedback beyond simple "right or wrong."

Fluency Tracking

Enforces a "Grace Period." Pausing too long drops your score, simulating real-world conversation pressure.

Universal Audio

Industrial-grade processor ingests and normalizes audio from any device, studio mic or smartphone.

Zero-Hallucination

Constrained alignment prevents "making up" words. Feedback is always grounded in the actual audio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered

How does the pronunciation analysis work?

OtterSpeak uses your browser's speech recognition to capture your voice during reading sessions. Otter Brain then compares your pronunciation against the expected words and provides detailed feedback on accuracy and any mispronounced sounds.

What happens if my Sun Energy runs out?

When your Sun Energy is depleted, you won't be able to start new sessions until it resets. Energy resets daily at midnight (your local time). You can upgrade to Happy or Prime for more daily energy, or wait for the reset.

How do streak freezes work?

Streak Freezes automatically protect your streak when you miss a day. If you don't complete a session, a freeze is used (if available) to maintain your streak. Happy Otters get 3 freezes/month, Prime gets 10. You can also purchase more from the Freeze Store.

Can I use OtterSpeak without a microphone?

Yes, but with limited functionality. You can still use the reading modes for speed reading practice, but you won't receive pronunciation analysis or accuracy scores. A microphone is recommended for the full experience.

What browsers are supported?

OtterSpeak works best on Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave) due to better speech recognition support. Firefox is supported but may have limited speech recognition. Safari has partial support.

Is there a mobile app?

OtterSpeak is currently a web-based Progressive Web App (PWA). You can add it to your home screen on mobile devices for an app-like experience. Native mobile apps are planned for the future.

Is my data secure?

Yes! User accounts and progress are stored securely. Session audio is processed in real-time and is not permanently stored. Your reading history retention depends on your plan tier (last 5 sessions for NPC, last 30 for Happy, unlimited for Prime).

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time from Account Settings. You'll continue to have access to premium features until your current billing period ends. No questions asked, no hassle.

What is Psycho Mode and is it safe?

Psycho Mode is an extreme reading challenge with chaotic visual effects and sound. It includes flashing lights and rapid movements. It is NOT recommended for users with photosensitivity, epilepsy, or motion sickness. You can disable it in Options → Accessibility → Photosensitivity Mode.

How do I contact support?

You can reach us through the Discord community (all users), or via direct feedback channels (Happy/Prime subscribers). Prime users have access to priority support.
